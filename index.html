Personal rclone Google Drive Integration

This page describes a privately operated Google OAuth integration used with rclone, an open-source file management tool, to access the owner's Google Drive account.

Purpose

The integration authorizes rclone to access Google Drive files as permitted by the account owner. File listing, transfers, and other operations are initiated by the owner through rclone on their own devices.

Who can use it?

This integration is intended for personal use by its operator. It is not offered as a hosted file-storage or file-processing service to the public.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for details about authorization, local token storage, and data access.
